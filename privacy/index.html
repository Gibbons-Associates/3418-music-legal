Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 3418 Music CLI (the “Application”), operated for 3418 Music, handles information when authorized 3418 Music personnel use its Meta integration.

What the Application does

The Application is an internal command-line and MCP integration for the Meta Marketing API. It is used to read and manage 3418 Music business advertising and related assets, including ad accounts, campaigns, ad sets, ads, creatives, Pages, catalogs, product sets/items/feeds, datasets or pixels, and advertising performance insights.

Information accessed from Meta

When an authorized operator uses the integration, Meta may provide business and advertising information needed for the requested operation. Depending on the operation, this can include asset names and IDs, account and campaign settings, delivery status, budgets, creative and catalog metadata, Page information, dataset or pixel metadata, and performance metrics such as spend, impressions, clicks, CTR, and CPC.

The Application is not a public consumer account service. Based on its current implementation, it does not create user profiles, collect passwords, or maintain a separate end-user account database.

How information is used

Information is used only to operate, monitor, reconcile, and report on 3418 Music’s authorized Meta business assets and advertising activity. Requests are sent to Meta through Meta’s APIs and are subject to Meta’s terms and policies.

Storage and retention

Meta credentials are kept in restricted private operational storage and are not published on this website. The Application’s repository does not contain access tokens. Command results may be displayed to an authorized operator, and write actions may be recorded in an internal audit log with the command, affected resource identifier, and reason. Records are retained only as needed for operations, security, and audit purposes; Meta separately controls its own retention under Meta’s policies.

Sharing

The Application does not sell personal information. Information is shared with Meta when required to use the requested Meta API operation, and with authorized 3418 Music operators and service providers acting on 3418 Music’s behalf when necessary for those operations. No other sharing is performed by the public legal pages.

Security

Access is limited to authorized operators and restricted operational environments. No security method is guaranteed to be perfect, so suspected unauthorized access should be reported promptly.

Your choices and deletion requests

To request deletion of information associated with the Meta integration, follow the instructions on the User Data Deletion page. You may also contact marketing@3418music.com.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the Application or its data practices change. The effective date above will identify the current version. Questions about this policy may be sent to marketing@3418music.com.
