User Data Deletion

Effective date: September 3, 2026

To request deletion: email marketing@3418music.com with the subject “Meta data deletion request.”

What to include

Include the name or email address associated with the Meta account used with the 3418 Music CLI integration, and identify the Facebook Page, Instagram account, ad account, or other 3418 Music business asset involved. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or other credentials.

What we will do

We will verify the request, identify information held in the Application’s restricted operational records, and delete or de-identify information that is eligible for deletion. If information must be retained for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or an internal audit record, we will limit its use and retain it only for that purpose.

If the requested information is held by Meta rather than by 3418 Music, we will explain that distinction and direct you to Meta’s own account and privacy controls. Deleting information from 3418 Music does not delete information held independently by Meta.

Response

We will acknowledge a complete request and communicate the outcome using the contact method provided. Requests are handled by 3418 Music personnel; this page does not collect data through a web form.

For general privacy questions, contact marketing@3418music.com or review the Privacy Policy.
